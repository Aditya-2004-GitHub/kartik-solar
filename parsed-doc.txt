Our Solar Services 
1. Solar EPC Services 
Complete solar project planning, engineering, procurement, and installation under one 
service. We handle everything from system design to final commissioning for residential, 
commercial, and industrial solar projects. 
2. Solar System Installation 
Professional installation of rooftop solar systems using high-quality solar panels, inverters, 
and mounting structures to ensure safe and efficient energy generation. 
 
 
 
3. MSEDCL Service Work 
Support for electrical services and solar approvals related to Maharashtra State Electricity 
Distribution Company Limited (MSEDCL) including net-metering applications, meter 
connections, and required documentation. 
4. Solar Consultation 
Expert guidance to help customers select the right solar system based on their electricity 
usage, budget, and property type to maximize savings and efficiency.

Company Introduction 
KARTIK MULTI SOLUTIONS is a solar EPC service provider based in Nagpur, offering 
solar energy solutions and service-based work related to MSEDCL. 
Company History 
Established in 2009, the company has been providing professional service solutions in the 
energy and electrical sector. With growing demand for renewable energy, the company 
expanded into solar EPC services to support clean and sustainable power solutions. 
 
 
 
Mission 
To provide reliable and affordable solar energy solutions while helping customers reduce 
electricity costs and adopt renewable energy. 
Vision 
To become a trusted solar service provider known for quality work, reliable installations, and 
customer satisfaction. 
 
Our Strengths

Experienced technical team 
•

• 
Reliable EPC project execution 
• 
Customer-focused approach 
• 
Support for residential and commercial projects
